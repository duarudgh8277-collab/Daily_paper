## Nature Communications

### Universal Planckian dissipation in the strange metal state of the cuprates
**저자**: A. Shekhter, B. J. Ramshaw, M. K. Chan, N. Harrison · **게재일**: 2026-07-26 · [원문 링크](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-75673-7)

**Abstract (EN)**: Nature Communications, Published online: 26 July 2026; doi:10.1038/s41467-026-75673-7 Through an analysis of existing data, the authors show that the “Planckian” relaxation rate in cuprate superconductors is doping independent. This follows from the finding that the square optical plasma frequency scales linearly with doping, which they further argue is a natural consequence of Mott physics.
