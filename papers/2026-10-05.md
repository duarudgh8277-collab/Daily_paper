**Abstract (EN)**: Nature Communications, Published online: 04 October 2026; doi:10.1038/s41467-026-78452-6 The second messenger c-di-GMP inhibits sporulation in the pathogenic bacterium Clostridioides difficile through unclear mechanisms.
