### Region-specific generation and retention of acetylated cohesin shape the genome-wide pattern of sister chromatid cohesion
**게재일**: 2026-10-31 · [원문 링크](https://academic.oup.com/nar/article/doi/10.1093/nar/gkag1061/9005799?rss=1)

**Abstract (EN)**: Abstract Sister chromatid cohesion depends on ESCO2-mediated acetylation of the cohesin subunit SMC3, yet how this modification is established and maintained across the genome remains poorly understood. Here, we examined the chromatin-binding dynamics of ESCO2 and its enzymatic activity using quantitative ChIP-seq of human cells synchronously progressing through S phase. ESCO2 bound broadly along chromosomes without forming discrete peaks and preferentially localized to transcriptionally inactive regions, but was not confined to H3K9me3- or H3K27me3-enriched heterochromatin. Consistent with this binding pattern, ESCO2-dependent SMC3 acetylation showed a similarly broad distribution in these regions. In contrast, ESCO1, a paralog of ESCO2, primarily mediated SMC3 acetylation at cohesin peak sites. Notably, most of the broadly distributed, ESCO2-dependent SMC3 acetylation generated during S phase was progressively lost from chromatin after replication, whereas the fraction that persisted into late G2 accumulated in domains enriched for the repressive H3K27me3 modification. Our work identifies selective post-replicative retention of acetylated cohesin as a previously unappreciated step in shaping the genome-wide architecture of sister chromatid cohesion.

### Integrator complex removes lesion-stalled RNA polymerase II after transcription-coupled repair to facilitate transcription restart
**게재일**: 2026-10-31 · [원문 링크](https://academic.oup.com/nar/article/doi/10.1093/nar/gkag1068/9005798?rss=1)

**Abstract (EN)**: Abstract Gene transcription by RNA Polymerase II (Pol II) is highly vulnerable to transcription-blocking DNA lesions (TBLs), which, if unresolved, induce transcription stress leading to cellular decline and aging. TBLs are removed by transcription-coupled nucleotide excision repair (TC-NER). TC-NER is initiated when Cockayne syndrome group B protein recognizes lesion-stalled Pol II and promotes TC-NER complex assembly. This complex recruits TFIIH for lesion verification, followed by incision and repair to enable transcription restart. Many TBLs stall Pol II within its active site, shielding the lesion from repair and requiring Pol II processing to expose it. However, whether this occurs through Pol II backtracking or release from the DNA, and the regulatory mechanisms involved, remain poorly defined. Here, we identified the Integrator complex as a key factor in lesion-stalled Pol II processing. Interaction proteomics revealed Integrator recruitment to late-stage TC-NER complexes. Integrator’s endonuclease core promotes efficient transcription recovery after TBL induction, without affecting lesion removal by TC-NER. Live-cell imaging and chromatin retention assays demonstrated that Integrator facilitates Pol II release from DNA following TC-NER. Collectively, these findings support a two-step mechanism for Pol II clearance during TC-NER, wherein TFIIH-mediated backtracking exposes the lesion for repair, followed by Integrator-mediated Pol II release to restore transcription.

### Queuosine-modification dependent histidyl tRNA fragment promotes cell-type dependent proliferation
**게재일**: 2026-10-31 · [원문 링크](https://academic.oup.com/nar/article/doi/10.1093/nar/gkag1101/9005797?rss=1)

**Abstract (EN)**: Abstract tRNA fragments (tRFs) are natural, small non-coding RNAs produced by endogenous ribonucleolytic cleavage of tRNAs. tRFs play a role in many biological pathways, including regulation of cell proliferation. Specific tRNA modifications affect tRF biogenesis. Queuosine (Q) modification occurs at the wobble anticodon nucleotide of 4 mammalian tRNAs and protects these tRNAs from fragmentation. Here, we investigated cell-type and tRNA Q-modification dependencies on mammalian cell proliferation. We found that Q-modified tRNA can either increase, decrease, or make no difference in proliferation. Proliferation decreased in MCF7, a breast cancer cell line, when tRNAs were Q-modified and dependent on the modification enzyme complex, QTRT1/2. MCF7 proliferation increases upon the transfection of Q-modification-dependent tRF His or decreases upon knockdown of endogenous tRF His . Consistent with Q-dependent proliferation reduction, polysome profiling showed decreased translation efficiency of ribosomal proteins. Finally, protein pull-down of MCF7 lysates using tRF His identified Musashi RNA-binding protein 2 (Msi2) as a tRF His -binding protein. Msi2 knockdown reduced the Q-modification-dependent or tRF His dependent cell proliferation effect. Our results illustrate a highly cell-type-dependent proliferation effect of tRNA Q-modification, suggesting multi-faceted mechanisms in studying this ubiquitous tRNA modification.
