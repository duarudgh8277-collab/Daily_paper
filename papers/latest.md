### HM-DyadCap – capture and mapping of 5-hydroxymethylcytosine/5-methylcytosine CpG dyads in mammalian DNA

**Abstract (EN)**: Abstract 5-Methylcytosine (mC) and 5-hydroxymethylcytosine (hmC) are the main epigenetic modifications of mammalian DNA, and play crucial roles in cell differentiation, development, and tumorigenesis.
