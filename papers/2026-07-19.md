**Abstract (EN)**: The partially genetic nature of post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD) emerges from both twin and recent molecular genetics studies and complicates the assessment of unique environmental influence on altered phenotypes in PTSD. Previous functional neuroimaging studies have reported heightened amygdalar responsivity in PTSD, but whether this abnormality relates to familial factors or PTSD-triggering environmental experiences is not fully understood.
