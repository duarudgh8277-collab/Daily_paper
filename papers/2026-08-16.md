### Depletion of davemaoite in Earth’s ambient lower mantle
**저자**: Ming Hao, Motohiko Murakami, Pinku Saha, Nicolas Guignot · **게재일**: 2026-08-15 · [원문 링크](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-76943-0)

**Abstract (EN)**: Nature Communications, Published online: 15 August 2026; doi:10.1038/s41467-026-76943-0 Davemaoite remains cubic throughout most of the lower mantle and exhibits intrinsically low shear-wave velocities. Seismic constraints indicate that it is depleted in the ambient lower mantle but enriched within LLSVPs, likely as a result of late-stage crystallization during magma-ocean differentiation.
