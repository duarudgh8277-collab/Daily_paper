**Abstract (EN)**: Nature Communications, Published online: 31 May 2026; doi:10.1038/s41467-026-73939-8 Understanding the structural origins of stability in disordered solids is essential for predicting mechanical failure and manipulating their properties, yet the inherent disorder complicates this analysis.
